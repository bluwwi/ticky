## Keyboard
- Pack: "Orange deluxe - Press + Release separated" by **jawsken123**.
- Edited from "Orange deluxe" by the same author, published on Mechvibes:
  https://mechvibes.com/sound-packs/orange-deluxe
- Source recording: a mix of C3 Tangerine and Tecsee Carrot switches (see the pack's own
  description for the original video links).
- The Mechvibes `config.json` timing map was used to slice the 13 unique key-press takes
  (`samples/down-01..13.wav`), 12 key-release takes (`samples/up-01..12.wav`), and the
  Enter/Backspace takes (`down-12.wav` / `down-10.wav`) from `audio.ogg`.
- Takes were downmixed to mono, resampled to 44.1 kHz 16-bit WAV, and leveled.

## Mouse
- Pack: "incott g23 v2" by **fastglocks** (Mechvibes mouse config).
- The four left/right click takes were sliced from `mouse.wav` using the config's timings,
  downmixed to mono, resampled to 44.1 kHz 16-bit WAV, and leveled
  (`mouse/left-1.wav`, `mouse/left-2.wav`, `mouse/right-1.wav`, `mouse/right-2.wav`).
- Middle/back/forward reuse those clicks. All four wheel directions deliberately reuse the
  Enter key sound (`samples/down-12.wav`), as requested.

Both source packs were supplied by the user in `assets/zips/`.
