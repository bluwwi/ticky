# Credits - Cherry Blue

All audio in this pack is CC0 (public domain).

- Original recording: **"Mechanical Keyboard 'Cherry Blue Switches'"** by **EricsSoundschmiede**.
  Source: https://freesound.org/s/382057/ - Creative Commons CC0 1.0.
- Individual strikes were detected, cut and leveled by the **Keyslam** project, distributed under
  CC0 1.0 at https://github.com/gumruyanzh/keyslam-packs (file: `examples/mx-blue`).
- Ticky conversion: takes were downmixed to mono, resampled to 44.1 kHz 16-bit WAV, and packed as
  `down-01..18` (medium/hard/slam), `enter-01..12` (wide keys), and `up-01..06` (releases).
- Mouse click and wheel sounds (`mouse/*.wav`) are short derivatives of these same CC0 takes,
  pitched, trimmed and leveled to roughly -3 dB for Ticky.

No attribution is required for CC0 audio, but the original recordist and Keyslam are credited here.
